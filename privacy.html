Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Overview

This application is a private, single-user tool used by its owner to publish content to the owner's own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the application handles.

2. Data collection

None. The application does not collect, store, or process personal data from any user other than the account owner. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no cookies.

3. Authentication tokens

Platform access tokens are generated through the official OAuth authorization of each platform and are stored only on the owner's private infrastructure, protected by file-system permissions. Tokens are never transmitted to third parties and can be revoked at any time from the platform's account settings.

4. Third parties

The application communicates only with the official APIs of social platforms, for the sole purpose of publishing the owner's own content.
